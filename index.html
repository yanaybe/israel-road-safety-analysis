Where: 1-km interurban segments

Excess risk = fatal and serious crashes per year above what a segment of the same road class and traffic would expect (empirical Bayes). It is the number an engineering fix can remove. Total ranks by expected crashes, which favours the busiest roads.

Source. Central Bureau of Statistics, “Road accidents with casualties, Public Use File”, 2020–2024, via data.gov.il (open licence). One row per crash; no personal identifiers.

Method. Segments are 1 km of a numbered interurban road. Light-injury crashes stand in for traffic volume, since no public per-segment traffic count exists. Rankings were validated on held-out years: the excess-risk list caught more of the next years' serious crashes than a list built from raw counts.
