## Q1: Are Israel's roads getting safer?
- Injury crashes -23% (2020 → 2024), but fatal crashes +42% and serious-injury crashes +29%. Light-injury crashes -37%.
- Excluding Judea & Samaria, fatal crashes +47%.
- 90.8% of the rise in fatal crashes is on interurban roads.
